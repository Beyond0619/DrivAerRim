## Third-party source material

The rim geometries incorporated into DrivAerRim were adapted from the [DeepWheel dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KAIST-SmartDesignLab/DeepWheel), created by Soyoung Yoo and Namwoo Kang and released under CC BY-NC 4.0. The source STL meshes were repaired, scaled, and integrated into the DrivAer wheel assemblies before simulation. The non-commercial condition is therefore retained in DrivAerRim.
